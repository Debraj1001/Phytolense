> 2. CHALLENGES & THE CRISIS

**Main heading:**
> THE PROBLEM WE'RE SOLVING

**Body paragraph (stat-driven, like MindfulConnect's 41% stat):**
> Crop disease is responsible for 20–40% of global agricultural yield loss
> every year, costing farmers billions. A 2022 FAO report reveals that
> smallholder farmers — who produce 70% of the world's food — lack access
> to timely expert diagnosis and are forced to rely on guesswork,
> leading to over-treatment, economic loss, and food insecurity.

**Two-column problem cards:**

**Card 1:**
> 1. Late Detection
> By the time a disease is visible to the naked eye,
> it has already spread to neighboring plants —
> causing irreversible damage to entire crop rows.

**Card 2:**
> 2. No Expert Access
> Rural farmers rarely have access to agronomists.
> Expert consultations are costly, slow, and often
> unavailable in local languages.
